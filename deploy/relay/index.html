relay.aidaemon.ai

This server helps AIDaemon apps reach your daemon from anywhere: your phone on mobile data, your laptop at a café, your daemon at home. Everything that passes through it is end-to-end encrypted, so the relay can't read it.

What it does

  1. listen

    Your daemon keeps one connection open here, so your devices can find it by its key. No accounts, no sign-up.

  2. connect

    An app asks for that key. The relay passes the first messages along while the two sides learn each other's addresses.

  3. direct

    Then they connect directly when the network allows, and the relay steps out of the way.

  4. carry

    When a direct path isn't possible, the relay forwards the traffic. It sees only ciphertext: QUIC with TLS 1.3 and keys only your devices hold.

Run your own

This relay is the default, never a requirement. Point your daemon at a relay you run and your devices use only that one.

# config.toml
[client_gateway.remote]
relays = ["https://relay.example.com"]
